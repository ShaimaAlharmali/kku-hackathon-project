Project option: Job application tracker
Problem: Students, recent graduates, and job seekers can lose track of saved opportunities, application stages, dates, and appropriate follow-ups across a long job search.
What I built: SAAY (سعي), a responsive browser-only application tracker with saved-versus-submitted records, stage/date tracking, search/filter/sort, 14-day follow-up attention rules, follow-up history, My Next Move guidance, official employer links, and a transparent pasted-text opportunity review.
Result: A polished, accessible working prototype that opens directly from index.html, persists data locally in the browser, includes clearly labelled made-up demo records, and does not use accounts, external APIs, job boards, email, AI, or hiring predictions.
